CP-80 baseline

tools/cp_model.py, from the service manual and physics only (no recordings yet). Strings, hammer, bridge piezo, the preamp fitted to the manual's calibration levels, the 4 kHz Sallen-Key, the antiphase tremolo.

Keys x velocity

A0 A1 A2 C4 A4 C6 A6 C8, each at v40 / v80 / v127 (2 s held, then the damper; A#6 up is undamped).

Progression

Cmaj7 Fmaj7 G7 C, then a line over C. Tone knobs at 5, BRILLIANCE MEDIUM.

Tremolo

The same, TREMOLO 5 Hz, DEPTH max. Stereo: ch1 left, ch2 right (antiphase). Use headphones.

BRILLIANCE HIGH

+5.5 dB at 5 kHz over MEDIUM.

BRILLIANCE LOW

-12 dB at 5 kHz under MEDIUM.

CP-70 filter

The same strings through the CP-70B's low-pass (2.75 kHz, Q 0.74) and its own tone calibration, no BRILLIANCE.
