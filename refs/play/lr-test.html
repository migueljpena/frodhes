Left-Right Test

This page does not use the Wurli model: these are pure tones from the browser, the same on both channels on BOTH. If a note leans to one side here too, the cause is in the gear, the room or the ear, not in the Wurli.

Playing: —

SWEEP

A tone rising slowly from 110 Hz to 1400 Hz (A2 to F6), in the center, over 40 s. Listen for any moment it leans to one side and comes back.
