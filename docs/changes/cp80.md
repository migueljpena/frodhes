## New
- **The CP-80 (TYPE 6).** A Yamaha CP-80 electric grand, computed from its strings: all 88 keys, one wound string a key in the low bass and two a few cents apart above (their gentle beating), the hammer simulated against the string (soft notes round, hard ones shorter and brighter), each overtone fading in two stages, no dampers from A#6 up, the piezo pickups under the bridge. Then the CP-80's own preamp and tremolo: VOICE 1/2 BASS, MIDDLE, TREBLE (5 the service manual's calibration) and BRILLIANCE (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH); VOICE 2/2 TREMOLO, DEPTH, SPEED (its two outputs in antiphase: left and right) and TUNE. Sustain with half-pedal, release velocity and pitch bend as on the other instruments; 10 voices. Over MIDI: CC 79 selects it.
## Tools
- `tools/cp_model.py` (the float reference) and `tools/cp_multirate.py` (the engine's algorithm in float: each overtone at the lowest of five rates its frequency allows, a pair of strings as one resonator with its beat, the overtones 50 dB under left out; -40 to -48 dB from the reference); `tools/gen_cp.py` -> `firmware/gen/cp_tables.h`; `tests/c/cp_ref.c`, `tests/py/test_cp_engine.py` (the C engine within -45 dB of the algorithm, the preamp within -50 dB, the tremolo within -45 dB), `tests/scripts/cp80.txt`.
